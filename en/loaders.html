How we price it

Repair or buy new?

01

We check the price of a new or used bucket with suppliers

We find the current price of a new and a used bucket for your machine and compare options on the market to see the real cost of replacement. That number is our baseline: a repair has to make economic sense

02

We assess how worn your bucket is

We inspect the metal, the geometry, the cutting edge, the mountings and other elements. We decide what truly needs restoring and what still has plenty of life. We never add work you do not need to the estimate

03

We calculate materials and labour

We count the steel, consumables and the scope of work. You get a clear restoration price before any work starts, so you know the budget up front and can compare it with the cost of a new bucket

04

You decide: repair or buy new

We show two numbers: what it costs to restore your bucket and what replacing it would cost. If a repair does not pay off, we say so directly. You choose the option that is better for your machine and your business
